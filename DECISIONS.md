# Decisions on Anchor Sentences

## Sentiment Anchoring Decision Protocol

The anchor set used in the sentiment analysis contains 12 positive and 12 negative sentences. The sentences were manually selected from the target corpus as short, relatively unambiguous examples of positive/restorative and negative/threatening affective language. The anchor texts are not included because they contain copyrighted material. Ambiguous, mixed, or strongly context-dependent sentences were excluded from the anchor set.

In regard to the sentence content itself, the protocol followed logic from past research on Tolkien's imagery and symbolism concerning evil and good, and positive and negative. Highly depending on Verlyn Flieger's *Splintered Light*, Tom Shippey's *J.R.R. Tolkien: Author of the Century* and Ursula K. Le Guin's *Rhythmic Pattern in The Lord of the Rings*, amongst other scholars.
